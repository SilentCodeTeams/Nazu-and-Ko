# Authorized use

Nazu&Ko is a protocol analysis and testing toolkit. It is built for:

- your own devices and lab networks
- security assessments with written authorization from the network owner
- defensive research and protocol education
- capture/analysis of traffic you are permitted to observe

It must not be used for:

- intercepting traffic on networks you do not own or have written permission
  to test
- ARP spoofing, flooding, or cache-poisoning against third parties
- controlling other people's devices
- credential theft, disruption, or evasion of network monitoring

## What the core intentionally does not include

- botnet or remote-control agents
- stealth/evasion features
- default-on remote administration
- exploit payloads

## Legal note

This is not legal advice. In China, unauthorized interception, control, or
disruption of computer systems can violate the Cybersecurity Law and the
Criminal Law (articles 285, 286 and 287-2). An open-source license or a
click-through agreement does not create authorization from a network owner and
does not remove criminal or civil liability.

If you are unsure whether you have authorization, stop and get it in writing.
